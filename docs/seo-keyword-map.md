## Publishing rules

- Keep one primary intent per page. Improve the mapped page before creating a new one.
- Use natural, descriptive links. Do not repeat exact-match anchors mechanically.
- Only publish local pages when the market has distinct, useful context. Do not mass-produce city pages.
- Articles should answer a real question and link to one relevant commercial next step.
- Case studies may state only work, services and results that are present in approved source material.
